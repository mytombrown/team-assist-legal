How to play

  • Plant the seeds. Grow the picture. Put your seeds on the plots, then tap the watering can.
  • Red poppies spread in a + shape. Blue cornflowers spread in an × shape. Yellow sunflowers jump two plots.
  • Everything grows at once. If two colors land on one plot, it turns to pebbles and nothing grows there.
  • Match the picture on the seed packet to clear the garden.

Hearts, coins and hints

  • Missed the picture? Try again costs a heart and keeps your seeds where they are. You hold up to 5; one comes back every 30 minutes.
  • You earn coins by playing. Spend them on hints (30 coins) and extra seeds (50 coins).

The shop

  • Coin packs: Handful, Basket, Wheelbarrow, Greenhouse.
  • Starter Bundle — coins, 5 hints and 3 extra seeds. One time only.
  • 1 Hour Endless Hearts — play all you want for an hour. Buy again to add an hour.
  • New device? Tap Restore purchases in the shop to get the Starter Bundle back.

Daily garden, scores and trophies

  • Daily garden: one new garden every day, with a few tries.
  • Signed in to Game Center? Your scores go on the all-time and daily leaderboards, and your trophies show as achievements.

Good to know

  • No ads, no accounts. The game plays offline.
  • Progress and coins are kept on the device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Game Center is optional. Turn it off in Settings › Game Center.
