Convert to PNG — Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026. Applies to the Convert to PNG app (com.thomasbrown.convert-to-png) by Tom Brown.

The short version
  • Your images never leave your device. Every conversion happens on it.
  • No network, no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.

Files

  • The app opens only the image files you drop on it or pick.
  • It writes the PNG copies to the output folder you choose. With Replace existing files off, it never overwrites a file.
  • Your original files are not changed, unless you turn on Replace and save a PNG over itself.

What the app stores

  • One thing: a bookmark to the output folder you chose, so the app can find it next time. It stays on this device.

What the app does NOT do

  • No network. The app makes no internet connections.
  • No third-party code. No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No permissions beyond the files and folder you pick.
  • No purchases.

Children

We collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Remove everything: delete the app. Your PNG files stay where you saved them.

Changes

If a future version ever sends anything off the device, this policy will change first and the release notes will say so.
