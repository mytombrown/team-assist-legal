Trainer Booking help

Need a hand?

Email appstoresupportwithasmile@icloud.com. Say whether you are a trainer or a parent, your phone, and what you were doing.

Trainers: get set up

  • Pick I'm a trainer and fill in your name and contact details.
  • Add sessions: private or group, the place, days, times, the age range you coach, and how many fit in a group.
  • Add special events (a camp or clinic). Parents send a request; you approve it.
  • Turn on 1-on-1 requests if you want parents to ask for a time that suits them.

Trainers: share your code

  • The My Code tab shows your code and a QR code. Send it to your families.
  • There is no public list of trainers. A family only finds you with your code.

Parents: book a session

  • Pick I'm booking for an athlete, add your name and your athlete's name.
  • Scan or type your trainer's code. Their page opens.
  • Tap Book a Session, pick a day and time, then Book it.
  • When a group is full it says Full. Two phones can never take the same spot.
  • Cancel from your bookings; the spot opens for someone else.
  • Android: the Trainer Booking parent app does the same.

Alerts

  • Trainers get an alert for a new booking, a cancel, or a request.
  • Parents get an alert when a request is answered, or if the trainer takes an athlete off a session (with the reason).
  • No alerts? Check Settings › Notifications › Trainer Booking.

Reports

  • The Reports tab shows a day, week or month: who booked, who came.
  • Export makes a spreadsheet file (CSV) you can share.

Trainer plan

  • Trainers get 30 days free, then $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year. Parents are always free.
  • If a plan ends, parents still see your page but cannot book until you subscribe.
  • Cancel any time: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Trainer Booking.
  • Terms: Apple's standard Terms of Use (EULA).

Your data

Schedules and bookings are kept on the Trainer Booking server so trainers and families can see them. No ads, no tracking. See the privacy policy. To delete anything, email us.
