Trainer Booking — Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026. Applies to the Trainer Booking app for iPhone (com.mytombrown.trainerbook) by Tom Brown.

The short version
  • We run no servers. Schedules and bookings are kept in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) so a trainer and their families can see them.
  • A booking is shared with the trainer you book. The names and contact details you type go to that trainer, nobody else.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no payments.

What the app stores, and where

WhatWhereWho can see it
Trainer's name, sessions, events, venues, prices, scheduleApple's iCloud (CloudKit public database), under the trainer's codeAnyone who has the trainer's code
Bookings: athlete's name, the session and timeApple's iCloud, under the trainer's codeThe trainer, and the family that booked
Event and 1-on-1 requests: athlete's name and age, parent's name, phone, email, messageApple's iCloud, under the trainer's codeThe trainer the request is sent to
Your own settings, venue history, saved codesThis device onlyOnly you

iCloud is Apple's service. Records are reached only by the trainer's code; there is no public list or search of trainers or athletes.

Camera

  • Used only to scan a trainer's code. No photo is saved or sent.

Notifications

  • Trainers get a notification when a session is booked or cancelled, or a request comes in. Apple's push service delivers it.

Maps

  • Searching for a venue address uses Apple Maps. The text you search is sent to Apple to find the place.

What the app does NOT do

  • No servers of ours.
  • No third-party tracking code. No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No payments. Prices are shown only; any payment happens between you and the trainer.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Children

The app is used by trainers and parents. A parent enters the athlete's name and age to book; that is shared only with the trainer they book.

Your choices

  • Cancel a booking in the app; it is removed from iCloud.
  • Camera and notifications: turn them off any time in Settings.
  • Remove anything else (a trainer's schedule, a request): email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If a future version collects anything else, this policy will change first and the release notes will say so.
