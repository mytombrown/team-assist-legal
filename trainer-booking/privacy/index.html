Trainer Booking — Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026. Applies to the Trainer Booking app for iPhone (com.mytombrown.trainerbook) and the Trainer Booking parent app for Android, by Tom Brown.

The short version
  • Schedules and bookings are kept on our server (trainerbook-api.mytombrown.com), so a trainer and their families can see them on iPhone and Android.
  • A booking is shared with the trainer you book. The names and contact details you type go to that trainer, nobody else.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Trainers pay through Apple; we never see card details.

There are no accounts and no sign-in. A phone gets a random ID the first time it opens the app; it is not tied to your name, Apple ID or Google account. Records are reached only by the trainer's code; there is no public list or search of trainers or athletes.

Our server

  • Runs on Amazon Web Services (Lightsail) in the United States. All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Like any web server, it sees your phone's IP address when it connects. We do not use it to identify you.
  • It keeps a short technical log: which request, when, and whether it worked.
  • A backup of the data is made each night. The newest 14 are kept; older ones are deleted.

Subscriptions

  • Trainers subscribe in the iPhone app through Apple's in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never see card or billing details.
  • Parents never pay and never see a subscription.
  • Terms of Use: Apple's standard license agreement (EULA).

Camera

  • Used only to scan a trainer's code. No photo is saved or sent.

Notifications

  • Trainers get an alert when a session is booked or cancelled, or a request comes in. Parents get an alert when a request is answered or an athlete is taken off a session.
  • The alert text (for example the athlete's name and the session) is passed to Apple's push service for iPhone, or Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging for Android, to deliver it.

Maps

  • Searching for a venue address uses Apple Maps. The text you search is sent to Apple to find the place.

What the app does NOT do

  • No third-party tracking code. No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No payments between parents and trainers. Prices are shown only; any payment happens between you and the trainer.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone, beyond the delivery services named above.

Children

The app is used by trainers and parents. A parent enters the athlete's name to book; that is shared only with the trainer they book. The app does not ask for an athlete's age.

Your choices

  • Cancel a booking in the app; it is deleted from our server.
  • Camera and notifications: turn them off any time in Settings.
  • Delete anything else (a trainer's schedule, a request, a phone's alert history): email us and we will delete it. Copies in the nightly backups are gone within 14 days.

Changes

If a future version collects anything else, this policy will change first and the release notes will say so.
