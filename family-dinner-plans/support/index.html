Family Dinner Plans help

Need a hand?

Email appstoresupportwithasmile@icloud.com. Include your iOS version and what you were doing.

Meals

  • Add a meal from the Meals tab with ingredients, recipe, cooking time, and a photo.
  • Scan Recipe reads a photo of a recipe (cookbook page, card, screenshot) and fills the meal in for you. On Apple Intelligence devices the ingredients and steps are structured automatically.

Plan the week

  • Pick a meal for each day on the Plan tab. Choose dinner only, or breakfast, lunch, and dinner, in Settings.
  • On Apple Intelligence devices, the sparkles button suggests meals for empty days.

Groceries

  • One tap builds the week's shopping list. The same ingredient across meals is combined.
  • Checkmarks sync to the whole family and survive rebuilding the list after a plan change.

Family sharing

  • Settings › Invite Family sends an iCloud share link. Everyone who accepts sees the same meals, plan, and groceries.
  • Each person needs an iCloud account signed in on their device.
  • Sync between devices can take a minute or two.

Calendar

  • Pick a calendar in Settings and planned meals are added to it. Use a shared family calendar so everyone sees what's cooking.

Your data

Everything lives in your iCloud. We collect nothing. See the privacy policy.
