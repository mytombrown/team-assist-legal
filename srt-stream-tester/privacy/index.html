SRT Tester — Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026. Applies to the SRT Tester app for iPhone, iPad and Mac (com.mytombrown.SRTStreamTester) by Tom Brown.

The short version
  • We run no servers and collect nothing. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • The app only connects to the SRT address you type in. Video and audio go straight between your device and that address.
  • Saved connections sync through your own iCloud, so your iPhone, iPad and Mac share the list.

Network: only where you point it

  • The app opens an SRT connection to the host and port you enter. Nothing else is contacted by the app.
  • Receive plays the stream that address sends you. Send sends your camera and microphone to that address, only while a send session is running.
  • Live link figures (round-trip time, loss, bitrate and the rest) are worked out on your device from the connection. They are not sent anywhere.
  • If you set a passphrase, the stream is encrypted with it (AES) between the two ends.

Camera and microphone

  • Asked for only when you start a Send session. They are the test source for the outgoing stream.
  • Nothing is recorded or saved. Picture and sound go only to the address you connected to.
  • Say no and Receive mode still works.

What the app stores, and where

A connection holds what you typed: host, port, mode, latency, stream ID and passphrase. iCloud is Apple's service, tied to your Apple Account. We cannot see it.

What the app does NOT do

  • No servers of ours.
  • No third-party tracking code. No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The streaming engine is open source (HaishinKit and libsrt), listed in the app's About screen.
  • No recording. Streams are played or sent live, never saved.
  • No purchases.

Children

SRT Tester is a tool for broadcast and streaming work. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Camera and microphone: turn them off any time in Settings › Privacy & Security (iPhone/iPad) or System Settings › Privacy & Security (Mac).
  • Saved connections: delete any one in the app. The delete reaches your other devices too.
  • Stop iCloud sync: turn off iCloud for apps in your Apple Account settings.
  • Remove everything: delete the app.

Changes

If a future version ever sends anything else off the device, this policy will change first and the release notes will say so.
