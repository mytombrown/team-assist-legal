SRT Tester help

Need a hand?

Email appstoresupportwithasmile@icloud.com. Include your device, its OS version, and what the other end of the link is (encoder, server or app).

Connect

  • Follow the guided setup, or paste an srt://host:port?… address and the fields fill in.
  • Version 1.0 connects as a caller: the other end must be listening. Listener mode is coming in a future update.
  • Pick Receive to watch the stream, or Send to send your camera and microphone as a test source.

Passphrase and stream ID

  • A passphrase must be 10–79 characters and match on both ends.
  • If the passphrase is wrong, the app says so: packets arrive but cannot be decrypted.
  • Some servers need a stream ID. Ask whoever runs the server for it.

Reading the numbers

  • The health line at the top gives one verdict: good, or what is wrong.
  • Tap any figure (hover on the Mac) for a plain-words explanation.
  • Rule of thumb: latency should be at least 3–4 times the round-trip time.

Ad cues (SCTE-35)

  • The Ad cues card shows each cue as it arrives, in plain words.
  • In Send mode you can insert a test cue into your outgoing stream.
  • The verdict line names what is missing if cues do not arrive.

Can't connect?

  • Check the host and port, and that the other end is listening.
  • Firewalls must allow UDP on that port.
  • Saved connections sync to your other devices through iCloud. Give it a minute.
