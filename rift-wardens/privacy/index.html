Rift Wardens — Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026. Applies to the Rift Wardens iPhone and iPad app (com.mytombrown.riftwardens) by Tom Brown.

The short version
  • We collect nothing. The app has no server and no networking code of its own.
  • Daily Challenge scores can go to Apple's Game Center — only if you are signed in to Game Center. Say no and the game plays the same.
  • Everything else stays on your phone.
  • No accounts of ours. No ads. No purchases. No tracking. No analytics. No permissions.

What the app stores, and where

Everything is on the phone, in the app's own settings storage. No names, ages or other personal details are asked for or stored.

WhatWhereWhy
Boards cleared, best points per board, XP and tower levels, rifts sealed, wins, wardens met, field reportsThis phone onlyKeep your place
Endless runs and Daily Challenge runs, with their scoresThis phone onlyYour own best scores
The look you picked and other settingsThis phone onlyRemember them

Game Center

  • The app asks Apple's Game Center to sign in. This is Apple's own screen and account, not ours.
  • If you are signed in, the app sends your Daily Challenge score to that day's leaderboard. Apple shows it with your Game Center nickname.
  • If you say no, nothing is sent and the game plays the same.
  • Leaderboards only: no friends list, no invites, no chat, no multiplayer.

Ratings

After some wins the app may show Apple's own "rate this app" prompt. Apple runs it; we receive nothing from it except a public rating if you choose to leave one.

What the app does NOT do

  • No networking code of its own. Apart from Game Center scores, sent by iOS to Apple, nothing is uploaded or downloaded by the app. It plays offline.
  • No third-party code. No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs.
  • No purchases.
  • No permissions. The app never asks for the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or notifications.
  • No web views and no links out of the app.

Children

We collect no personal information from children (COPPA) or from anyone else. Deleting the app clears everything it keeps on the phone.

Your choices

  • Keep scores on the phone: decline the Game Center sign-in, or turn Game Center off in iOS Settings › Game Center.
  • Forget everything: delete the app. We hold no copy to delete. A score already on a Game Center leaderboard is Apple's; manage it in iOS Settings › Game Center.

Changes

If a future version ever sends anything else off the phone, this policy will change first and the release notes will say so.
