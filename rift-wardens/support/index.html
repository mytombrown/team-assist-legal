Need a hand?

Email appstoresupportwithasmile@icloud.com. Include your iPhone or iPad model, its iOS version, and the rift and board you were on.

How to play

  • Five rifts are tearing open. Build towers along the path to stop what comes through.
  • Dust Plains teaches the game in 10 boards. Every rift after it has 20.
  • When a new enemy is on its way, Commander Vale sends a field report: what it is and which tower beats it. Every report stays in your Field Reports list.
  • Play at 1x, 2x or 4x speed.

The rifts

  • Dust Plains — aliens.
  • Grave Rift — zombies.
  • Fang Rift — monsters.
  • Iron Rift — soldiers.
  • Hive Rift — giant bugs.

Towers

  • Machine Gun, Mortar Cannon, Rocket Launcher and Arc Coil in Dust Plains. Every rift adds one more: Flame Tower, Frost Cannon, Railgun, Flak Tower.
  • Each tower has three upgrade paths. Pick the one the next wave needs.
  • Selling a tower gives back 75% of everything spent on it, upgrades included. You confirm before it sells.

Endless and the Daily Challenge

  • Endless: enemies from every rift you've opened, harder every wave, until your base falls.
  • Daily Challenge: one new board every day, the same for everyone. Beat your best score as many times as you like. Signed in to Game Center? Your score goes on that day's leaderboard.

Looks

  • Play in lit 3D, or pick chunky 16-bit pixels, big flat blocks, or glowing arcade lines. Same game, your choice of screen.

Good to know

  • No ads, no accounts. The game plays offline.
  • Free: Dust Plains, Endless and the Daily Challenge. Grave, Fang, Iron and Hive are $0.99 each, or all 4 for $2.99, one time.
  • Bought a rift on another phone, or deleted the app? Tap a locked rift, then Restore Purchases (same Apple ID).
  • Progress is kept on the device. Deleting the app deletes it; rifts you bought come back with Restore Purchases.
  • Game Center is optional. Turn it off in Settings › Game Center.
