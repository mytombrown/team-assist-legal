Squishy Quest — Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026. Applies to the Squishy Quest iPhone app (com.mytombrown.squishyquest) by Tom Brown.

The short version
  • We collect nothing. The app has no server and no networking code.
  • No accounts. No ads. No tracking. No analytics. No purchases.
  • Your game stays on your phone, and deleting the app deletes it.
  • It asks for no permissions — no camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts.

What the app stores, and where

All of it is on the phone, in the app's own local storage (iOS UserDefaults). None of it is sent anywhere. No names, ages or other personal details are asked for or stored.

WhatWhyWhere in the code

What the app does NOT do

  • No networking code. Nothing is uploaded, downloaded or synced by the app. (Your own iCloud device backup may include the app's local data, under Apple's terms, like any other app.)
  • No third-party code. No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs.
  • No permissions. The app never asks for the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or notifications.
  • No purchases.
  • No web views. The only link out is Help on the title screen. It opens the help site in Safari, and only after a grown-up answers a sum.
  • Music and sound are made inside the app (no audio files, no streaming) and follow the phone's silent switch.

Children

Squishy Quest is made for families. We collect no personal information from children (COPPA) or from anyone else. A parent or guardian can clear everything the app remembers by deleting the app.

Your choices

  • Forget everything: delete the app. There is no copy anywhere else to ask us to delete.

Changes

If a future version ever sends anything off the device, this policy will change first and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
