Squishy Splat Defense — Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026. Applies to the Squishy Splat Defense iPhone app (shown on the phone as "Splat Defense", com.mytombrown.squishysplatdefense) by Tom Brown.

The short version
  • Nothing leaves your phone. The app has no internet connection of any kind.
  • No accounts. No sign-in. No ads. No tracking. No analytics. No purchases.
  • What it remembers stays on the device, and deleting the app deletes it.
  • It asks for no permissions — no camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts.

What the app stores, and where

All of it is on the phone, in the app's own local settings storage (iOS UserDefaults). None of it is sent anywhere. No names, ages or other personal details are asked for or stored.

What the app does NOT do

  • No network. There is no networking code in the app. Nothing is uploaded, downloaded, synced or backed up by the app itself. (Your own iCloud device backup may include the app's local data, under Apple's terms, like any other app.)
  • No third-party code. No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs.
  • No permissions. The app never asks for the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or notifications.
  • No purchases, no links out, no web views. Nothing in the app opens a browser or a store.
  • Sound effects are generated inside the app (no audio files, no streaming) and respect the phone's silent switch.

Children

Squishy Splat Defense is made for children. Because no information is collected or transmitted, the app does not collect personal information from children (COPPA) or from anyone else. A parent or guardian can clear everything the app remembers by deleting the app.

Your choices

  • Forget everything: delete the app. There is no server-side copy to ask us to delete, because there is none.

Changes

If a future version ever adds something that sends data off the device (for example Game Center high scores), this policy will change first and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
