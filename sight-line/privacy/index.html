Sight Line: Color Pop — Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026. Applies to the Sight Line iPhone app (com.mytombrown.sightline) by Tom Brown.

The short version
  • We collect nothing. The app has no server and makes no network calls.
  • Nothing leaves the phone. Your best score, your game in hand and your settings stay on the device.
  • No accounts. No ads. No tracking. No analytics. No purchases.
  • It asks for no permissions — no camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or notifications.

What the app stores, and where

All of it is on the phone, in the game page's own local storage inside the app. None of it is sent anywhere. No names, ages or other personal details are asked for or stored.

What the app does NOT do

  • No network calls. The game is one page built into the app. The typefaces are built in too. The app refuses to load anything that is not part of it.
  • No third-party code. No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs.
  • No purchases.
  • No permissions.
  • No links out of the app.
  • Sounds are made inside the app from tones (no audio files, no streaming).

Children

We collect no personal information from anyone, children included (COPPA).

Your choices

  • Forget everything: delete the app. We hold no copy to delete.

Changes

If a future version ever sends anything off the device, this policy will change first and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
