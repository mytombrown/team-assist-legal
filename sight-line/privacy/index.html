Sight Line: Color Pop — Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026. Applies to the Sight Line iPhone app (com.mytombrown.sightline) by Tom Brown.

The short version
  • We collect nothing. The app has no server and no networking code of its own.
  • Your save follows you through your own iCloud — best score, recent daily scores and settings. We cannot see it.
  • Scores can go to Apple's Game Center — only if you are signed in.
  • Purchases go through Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • No accounts of ours. No ads. No tracking. No analytics. No permissions.

What the app stores, and where

No names, ages or other personal details are asked for or stored.

iCloud

  • The save above is kept in iCloud key-value storage on your own Apple ID, so a new phone picks up where you left off.
  • Apple stores it. We have no access to it and no copy of it.
  • Signed out of iCloud, or iCloud off for the app? The save stays on the phone only.

Game Center

  • The app asks Apple's Game Center to sign in. This is Apple's own screen and account, not ours.
  • If you are signed in, the app sends your endless best and your daily score to two leaderboards, and shows the top of each board with players' Game Center nicknames.
  • If you say no, nothing is sent and the game plays the same.
  • Leaderboards only: no friends list, no invites, no chat, no multiplayer.

Purchases

  • Sight Line is a free download with a 3-day free trial and a one-time purchase, Sight Line Full, through Apple's In-App Purchase.
  • Apple handles the payment. We receive no card, name, email or address — only Apple's anonymous record that a purchase happened.
  • Restore asks Apple for your purchase again on a new phone.

What the app does NOT do

  • No networking code of its own. The game is one page built into the app, typefaces too; the app refuses to load anything that is not part of it. Only iOS talks to Apple for iCloud, Game Center and purchases.
  • No third-party code. No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs.
  • No permissions.
  • No links out of the app.
  • Sounds are made inside the app from tones (no audio files, no streaming).

Children

We collect no personal information from anyone, children included (COPPA).

Your choices

  • Keep scores on the phone: decline the Game Center sign-in, or turn Game Center off in iOS Settings › Game Center.
  • Keep the save off iCloud: iOS Settings › your name › iCloud, and turn Sight Line off.
  • Forget everything: delete the app, and remove its iCloud data in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage. We hold no copy to delete. A score already on a Game Center leaderboard is Apple's; manage it in iOS Settings › Game Center.

Changes

If a future version ever sends anything else off the device, this policy will change first and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
