Pip's Arcade — Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026. Applies to the Pip's Arcade iPhone app (com.mytombrown.squishyskypatrol) by Tom Brown: the DotVille town, the Dot claw, My Room, and the games inside it, Sky Patrol and Splat Defense. This replaces the earlier Squishy Sky Patrol policy.

The short version
  • We collect nothing. The app has no server and no networking code of its own.
  • Best scores can go to Apple's Game Center — only if you are signed in to Game Center. Say no and every game plays the same.
  • Game progress can go to your own iCloud, so your other iPhones on the same Apple Account carry on. It goes to Apple, never to us.
  • Tickets and prizes are not money. They are earned by playing and can never be bought. Claw tries are never sold.
  • Purchases go through Apple, behind a grown-up check. We never see a name, card or Apple Account.
  • No accounts of ours. No ads. No tracking. No analytics. No permissions.

What the app stores, and where

Everything is on the phone, in the app's own settings storage. No names, ages or other personal details are asked for or stored.

iCloud is Apple's key-value storage in your own account. Apple keeps it under its own privacy policy; we cannot read it. Purchases, tickets, prizes and My Room are not in it.

Game Center

  • The app asks Apple's Game Center to sign in. This is Apple's own screen and account, not ours.
  • If you are signed in, the app sends one number per game — your best score in Sky Patrol and in Splat Defense — to that game's leaderboard. Apple shows it with your Game Center nickname.
  • If you say no, nothing is sent and every game plays the same.
  • Leaderboards only: no friends list, no invites, no chat, no multiplayer.
  • A grown-up can turn Game Center off in iOS Settings › Game Center, or limit it with Screen Time.

Tickets, prizes and purchases

  • Tickets come from stars earned in the games. They buy prizes for My Room at the prize counter. They have no money value and cannot be bought.
  • The claw is free to play. Whether a grab holds is chance, as in a real claw machine, but nothing is paid for and tries are never sold.
  • Purchases: the first levels of every game land are free. A one-time $0.99 purchase per land opens the rest. Each purchase sits behind a grown-up check and goes through Apple's own purchase sheet. The app keeps only "this land is open".

What the app does NOT do

  • No networking code of its own. Apart from Game Center scores and the iCloud progress copy, both sent by iOS to Apple, nothing is uploaded or downloaded by the app.
  • No third-party code. No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs.
  • No permissions. The app never asks for the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or notifications.
  • No web views and no links out of the app.

Children

Pip's Arcade is made for children aged 9 to 11. We collect no personal information from children (COPPA) or from anyone else. The only things that can leave the phone are best scores to Apple's Game Center and game progress to the family's own iCloud, both controlled by a grown-up in iOS Settings. Deleting the app clears everything it keeps on the phone.

Your choices

  • Keep scores on the phone: decline the Game Center sign-in, or turn Game Center off in iOS Settings.
  • Keep progress on one phone: turn iCloud off for Pip's Arcade in iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud.
  • Forget everything: delete the app, and its iCloud copy in iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage. We hold no copy to delete. A score already on a Game Center leaderboard is Apple's; manage it in iOS Settings › Game Center.

Changes

If a future version ever sends anything else off the phone, this policy will change first and the release notes will say so.
