Welcome to DotVille

  • DotVille is Pip's little paper town. Every building is something to do.
  • Tap a building to go in: Sky Patrol, Splat Defense, the Claw Machine, Pip's Prize Counter or My Room.

Sky Patrol

  • Fly a paper plane and pop grumpy clouds until they cheer up.
  • 400 levels across Rainbow Skies, Cosmic Coast, Thunder Tops and Frosty Flight, with a boss at the end of every world.

Splat Defense

  • Baron Bleach and Count Socksworth want the town colorless.
  • Build paint towers along the path and splat the Blanks back into color. 200 levels in Paint Park and Spin Cycle.

The Claw Machine

  • Pip shows you how, then it's your turn. Line up the claw and grab.
  • The first Dot you win each day goes to your room. Win the same Dot again and it grows: small, medium, large.
  • More wins that day pay tickets. The Claw Machine is free to play.

Tickets, prizes and your room

  • Every mission pays tickets. More stars, more tickets. Beat your best and you get the difference.
  • Trade tickets at Pip's Prize Counter for Dots, hats for Pip, room decor and friends from DotVille Quest.
  • Tickets come from playing. They can never be bought.
  • Everything you win shows up in My Room. Drag anything where you like and it stays there. Tap a Dot or a friend to read who they are.
  • Stars from every game open hats for Pip. A new daily challenge adds bonus stars.

For grown-ups

  • No ads, no accounts, no links out of the app.
  • The first 10 levels of every land are free. More levels are one purchase per land, behind a grown-up check.
  • Bought a land on another device, or reinstalled? Tap Restore Purchases on the Pick a Land screen.
  • Game Center is optional. If your child is signed in, only the best score goes to the leaderboard. Turn it off in Settings › Game Center.
  • Progress is saved on the device and in your own iCloud.
