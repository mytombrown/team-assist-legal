How to play

  • Drag one thumb anywhere to fly Pip's paper plane.
  • The plane blows bubbles by itself. Pop the grumpy clouds to make them happy.
  • You have three hearts. Bumping a cloud, a raindrop or a boss costs one.
  • Pop a Gold cloud for a bounce shield that keeps you safe for a few seconds.

The clouds

  • Puff — one pop.
  • Drizzle — two pops. It zigzags.
  • Rumble — four pops. It hovers and drops raindrops.
  • Gold — drops the bounce shield.

The bosses

  • Level 1: Big Bubble Bob.
  • Level 4: Duchess Drizzle.
  • Level 7: Grumble Gus.
  • Level 10: Big Bubble Bob again, in a crown.
  • Each boss talks before the fight. Keep popping until their bar is empty.

Stars, coins and the map

  • 1 star for clearing a level. 2 for popping half the clouds. 3 for popping most of them with two hearts left.
  • Jelly coins come from every cloud you pop.
  • Clear a level and the next one opens on the map. Play any open level again to beat your stars.

For grown-ups

  • No ads, no purchases, no links out of the app.
  • Game Center is optional. If your child is signed in, only the best score goes to the leaderboard. Turn it off in Settings › Game Center.
  • Sound respects the silent switch.
  • Progress is kept on the phone. Deleting the app deletes it.
