How to play

  1. Each lamp shines at the wall across from it.
  2. Tap a floor square to add a block. Blocks cast shadows on both walls.
  3. Make both shadows match the two pictures.
  4. You only get a few blocks. One tall stack can fill both walls at once.
  5. Tap Lights on to check.

The first two rooms walk you through it. From room three you're on your own.

The five worlds

  • Classic — the rules above.
  • Stuck blocks — grey blocks are stuck where they are.
  • Holes — dark squares are holes; nothing stands there.
  • Glass — glass blocks cast no shadow.
  • Third light — a lamp overhead: match the floor too.

Each world has 20 rooms. A new world's first room explains its new rule.

Hearts, coins and hints

  • A wrong Lights on costs a heart and keeps your build. You hold up to 5; one comes back every 30 minutes.
  • You earn coins by playing. Spend them on hints (30 coins) and extra blocks (50 coins).

The shop

  • Coin packs: Candle, Lantern, Street Lamp, Lighthouse.
  • Night Owl Kit — coins, 4 hints and 4 extra blocks. One time only.
  • 1 Hour Endless Hearts — play all you want for an hour. Buy again to add an hour.
  • New phone? Tap Restore purchases in the shop to get the Night Owl Kit back.

Tonight's room, scores and trophies

  • Tonight's room: one new room every day, the same for everyone. No try limit — every flip of the switch is counted in your score.
  • Signed in to Game Center? Your scores go on the all-time and daily leaderboards, and your trophies show as achievements.

Good to know

  • No ads, no accounts. The game plays offline.
  • Progress and coins are kept on the phone. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Game Center is optional. Turn it off in Settings › Game Center.
