Two Lights — Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026. Applies to the Two Lights iPhone app (com.mytombrown.twolights) by Tom Brown. Two Lights is coming soon to the App Store.

The short version
  • We collect nothing. The app has no server and no networking code of its own.
  • Scores and trophies can go to Apple's Game Center — only if you are signed in to Game Center. Say no and the game plays the same.
  • Purchases go through Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Everything else stays on your phone.
  • No accounts of ours. No ads. No tracking. No analytics. No permissions.

What the app stores, and where

Everything is on the phone, in the app's own settings storage. No names, ages or other personal details are asked for or stored.

Game Center

  • The app asks Apple's Game Center to sign in. This is Apple's own screen and account, not ours.
  • If you are signed in, the app sends your scores to two leaderboards (all-time and daily) and your trophies as Game Center achievements. Apple shows them with your Game Center nickname.
  • If you say no, nothing is sent and the game plays the same.
  • Leaderboards and achievements only: no friends list, no invites, no chat, no multiplayer.

Purchases

  • The shop sells coin packs, the one-time Night Owl Kit and an hour of endless hearts through Apple's In-App Purchase.
  • Apple handles the payment. We receive no card, name, email or address — only Apple's anonymous record that a purchase happened.
  • Restore purchases in the shop asks Apple for your one-time purchases again.

What the app does NOT do

  • No networking code of its own. Apart from Game Center and Apple's In-App Purchase, both run by iOS, nothing is uploaded or downloaded by the app. It plays offline.
  • No third-party code. No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs.
  • No permissions. The app never asks for the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or notifications.
  • No web views and no links out of the app.

Children

We collect no personal information from children (COPPA) or from anyone else. Deleting the app clears everything it keeps on the phone.

Your choices

  • Keep scores on the phone: decline the Game Center sign-in, or turn Game Center off in iOS Settings › Game Center.
  • Turn off purchases: iOS Settings › Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions › iTunes & App Store Purchases.
  • Forget everything: delete the app. We hold no copy to delete. A score already on a Game Center leaderboard is Apple's; manage it in iOS Settings › Game Center.

Changes

If a future version ever sends anything else off the phone, this policy will change first and the release notes will say so.
