Need a hand?

Email appstoresupportwithasmile@icloud.com. Include your iPhone model, its iOS version, and the mission you were on.

How to fly

  • Drag one thumb anywhere to steer. The ship fires by itself.
  • Your hull takes three hits. A hit also takes your power-ups.
  • The Nova Bomb button bottom-left clears the screen, once a mission.

Power-ups

  • R — Rapid Fire: faster, then twin bolts, then three.
  • W — Wing Drones: one drone, then two, then they fire faster.
  • > — Speed Up: your ship covers more ground than your thumb.
  • S — Shield: nine seconds of cover, from the gold Carrier.
  • Lose one to a hit and it comes back later in the mission.

The galaxies and bosses

  • Helios Run: The Warden, then Void Marshal.
  • Ember Nebula: mines. Cinder, then Forge Titan.
  • Frost Veil: seekers. Icebreaker, then Glacier Queen.
  • Hive Cluster: lancers. Hive Guard, then Hive Mother.
  • Void Core: warpers. Eclipse, then Singularity.
  • Bosses wait at missions 5 and 10 of every galaxy. They get angry at half health.
  • Finish a galaxy to open the next one.

Stars and high scores

  • Up to three stars a mission.
  • Score counts ships destroyed, time under par, credits and hull left.
  • Your total is every mission's best, added up. Signed in to Game Center? It goes to the High Scores board.
  • Progress follows your Apple ID through iCloud to your other iPhones.
