Overlay Studio — Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026. Applies to the Overlay Studio app for Mac (com.mytombrown.overlaystudio) by Tom Brown.

The short version
  • Overlay Studio doesn't collect anything about you. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • Your templates and images stay on your Mac.
  • Graphics leave your Mac only through feed links you use, or through outside sharing if you turn it on.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your templates, buttons and images are saved on this Mac, inside the app's own folder.
  • Files you import (PNG, Photoshop) are copied into that folder. We never receive them.

What leaves your Mac, and only when you do it

  • Feed links. The app runs a small web server so OBS, vMix or a browser can show your graphics. Anyone on your network who has a link can see what's on air.
  • Share outside my network. Off until you turn it on. It connects your Mac to Cloudflare, which gives you a public link and passes your graphics through its network. Links carry a private key. Turn the switch off and the link stops working. Cloudflare's privacy policy covers that service.
  • Help. The Help menu opens help.mytombrown.com in your browser.

What the app does NOT do

  • No servers of ours.
  • No third-party tracking code. No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No purchases.

Children

Overlay Studio is a tool for broadcast and streaming work. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Outside sharing: turn the switch off and the link stops working.
  • Remove everything: delete the app and its folder.

Changes

If a future version ever sends anything else off your Mac, this policy will change first and the release notes will say so.
