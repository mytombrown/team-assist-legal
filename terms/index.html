Team Assist is a tool for coaches, trainers, parents, guardians, adult athletes, and athletes 15 to 17 that a parent adds. Use it honestly, keep your account to yourself, and treat the people you connect with respectfully.

1. Who may use Team Assist

Team Assist is for adults, and for athletes 15 to 17 that a parent adds. Coaches, trainers, parents, guardians, and adult athletes use Team Assist. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account, set up a family, or connect to a coach or trainer. An athlete aged 15 to 17 may use the app only on a family connection a parent or guardian added them to; the parent or guardian is responsible for that athlete's use and can remove the athlete's phone at any time. By using Team Assist you confirm that you are 18 or older, or that you are 15 to 17 and were added by your parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 15, and we do not collect dates of birth or ages for any athlete.

You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you enter and for keeping your sign-in credentials private. Use Team Assist only to organize training, scheduling, availability, assignments, and related communication. Do not use it to harass anyone, to submit information about another person without their authorization, or to attempt to access accounts or data that are not yours.

4. Content and communication

Messages, announcements, and training records you create are visible to the connected adults on the other side of each connection. Every chat participant is an adult, or an athlete 15 to 17 a parent added; nothing in a connection is hidden from the adult who claimed it.
