Know Your Music — Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026. Applies to the Know Your Music iPhone app (com.mytombrown.yeardrop) by Tom Brown.

The short version
  • We run no servers and collect nothing. No accounts of ours, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Your best scores and recent searches stay on your phone.
  • Songs come from Apple Music, and Versus runs through Apple's Game Center, both under Apple's privacy policy.

What the app stores, and where

On the phone only, in the app's own settings storage (iOS UserDefaults). It is not sent anywhere, and we cannot see it.

WhatWhyWhere in the code
Your best score for each decade, and for each genre or search you have played in it (bestScore.…) To show your best and celebrate a new one YearDrop/Game/BestScores.swift
Your last five Custom searches, as typed (customRecent) Quick picks under the Custom field YearDrop/App/AppModel.swift
The genre or search you picked last (selectedGenre) To start on it next time YearDrop/App/AppModel.swift

None of this includes your name, email, contacts or location. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Apple Music (Apple)

  • On your first game the app asks for access to Apple Music. It uses that access only to find songs and play them in the game, through Apple's MusicKit.
  • The app searches Apple Music's catalog for decade and genre playlists. If you type a Custom search, that text is sent to Apple Music's catalog search to find matching songs.
  • With an Apple Music subscription you hear the songs; without one, Apple's 30-second previews. Songs are streamed, never downloaded or kept.
  • The app does not read your library, playlists or listening history, and adds nothing to them. "Open in Apple Music" opens the song in Apple's Music app.
  • Apple runs Apple Music under its own terms and Apple's Privacy Policy. Turn access off any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Media & Apple Music; the game then uses built-in sample songs.

Game Center (Apple)

  • Game Center is used only for Versus: a live match between two players. If you are signed in to Game Center, you can invite a friend, send an invite in Messages, or be matched with anyone.
  • A Versus match carries only what the game needs, sent between the two phones through Game Center: your Game Center name, the decade and genre or search being played, the ten songs (Apple Music song IDs, titles, artists, years and artwork links), a timing value so both phones start each song together, each player's answer and score, and rematch or leave messages.
  • Other players see your Game Center nickname according to your Game Center settings. Apple runs Game Center under Apple's Privacy Policy; we do not receive your Apple Account details or the match messages.
  • Not signed in? The solo game works fully. Only Versus needs Game Center.

What the app does NOT do

  • No servers of ours. The only places data goes are Apple Music and, if you play Versus, Game Center — both Apple's.
  • No third-party code. No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs.
  • One permission only: Apple Music. The app never asks for the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or notifications.
  • No purchases today. If paid options are ever added, this policy will say so first.

Children

Know Your Music is a general-audience game. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children. Apple Music and Game Center have their own age rules and parental controls, set in iOS Screen Time.

Your choices

  • Apple Music access: iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Media & Apple Music.
  • Leave Game Center out: sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings, or never sign in. The solo game keeps working.
  • Forget everything on this phone: delete the app.
  • Game Center data: manage or remove it in iOS Settings › Game Center, under Apple's terms.

Changes

If a future version ever sends anything else off the device, or adds purchases, this policy will change first and the release notes will say so.
