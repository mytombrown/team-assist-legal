Recap help

Need a hand?

Email appstoresupportwithasmile@icloud.com. Include your device, its OS version, and what you tapped.

Record

  • Tap the red button. Recap keeps recording with the screen locked.
  • While it records: type notes, tap Highlight at a moment that matters, take a photo of a whiteboard.
  • Tap Stop. The transcript is made on your device.

From the lock screen

  • While recording, the lock screen and Dynamic Island show the time and four buttons.
  • Pause / Resume, Highlight, Photo, Stop — no need to unlock.
  • Photo opens the camera over the lock screen. The photo goes into the recording that is running.
  • Don't see it? Settings › Recap › Live Activities must be on.

Notes and podcast

  • Open a recording for its key points, decisions and to-dos.
  • Tap a point to hear the moment it was said.
  • Make a newscast or a two-voice podcast of it to listen back.

Things to know

  • Recap needs a device with Apple Intelligence.
  • Better voices: Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices, or turn on natural voices in Recap's Settings.
  • Sync with iCloud keeps recordings on your iPhone, iPad and Mac.
