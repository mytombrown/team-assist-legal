Recap — Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026. Applies to the Recap app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch (com.mytombrown.recap) by Tom Brown.

The short version
  • We run no servers and collect nothing. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Your recordings stay on your devices. The transcript, summary and podcast are made on the device by Apple's on-device speech and Apple Intelligence.
  • Two things leave the device, only if you turn them on: iCloud sync (your own iCloud Drive), and Claude or ChatGPT with your own key.

Microphone

  • Used only while you are recording. The sound is saved as a file in the app's folder on your device.
  • Turning speech into text happens on the device. Recap asks Apple's speech recognizer to stay on the device and never sends audio away.
  • On the Mac, Include call audio also records the Mac's own sound (the other side of a call). That needs the Screen & System Audio Recording permission and is off unless you turn it on.

Camera and photos

  • During a recording you can photograph a whiteboard or slide, from the app or from the lock screen. The photo is saved into that recording's folder.
  • Text in the photo is read on the device (Apple's Vision). It is used in that recording's notes.
  • Picking a photo from your library reads only the photo you pick.

Lock screen and Dynamic Island

  • While recording, a Live Activity shows the time and the Pause, Highlight, Photo and Stop buttons. It shows the recording's title and counts, nothing else.
  • Photos taken from the lock screen are kept by the system for Recap until Recap moves them into the recording.

iCloud is Apple's service, tied to your Apple Account. We cannot see it. Recordings are also listed in Spotlight and Siri on your device so you can find them by name.

When something leaves the device

  • Connect your AI (off by default). If you add your own Claude (Anthropic) or ChatGPT (OpenAI) key, the transcript, your notes and photo text of a recording you summarize are sent to that company to write the notes. Their own privacy policy covers what they do with it. Remove the key and nothing is sent.
  • Natural voices (off by default). Turning them on downloads the free Kokoro voice model once from Hugging Face. Nothing about you or your recordings is sent; it is a file download.
  • Sharing. Anything you share with the Share button goes where you send it.

What the app does NOT do

  • No servers of ours. No account to make.
  • No third-party tracking code. No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No purchases.

Children

Recap is a note-taking tool. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Microphone, camera, speech recognition: turn them off any time in Settings › Privacy & Security (iPhone/iPad) or System Settings › Privacy & Security (Mac).
  • Delete a recording in the app. Everything in its folder goes. Or turn on Delete recordings after 30 days.
  • Stop iCloud sync: turn off Sync with iCloud in Recap's Settings.
  • Remove everything: delete the app (and its iCloud Drive folder if you used sync).

Changes

If a future version ever sends anything else off the device, this policy will change first and the release notes will say so.
