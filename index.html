Email we send

Team Assist sends transactional email only.

Every message Team Assist sends is triggered by something the recipient did in the app, using an address they entered themselves. There are three kinds:

  • Account verification codes. A six-digit code sent when someone creates a Team Assist account, to confirm the address belongs to them.
  • Password reset codes. A six-digit code sent only when the account holder asks to reset their password.
  • Connection notices. Confirmation that a coach or trainer invitation has been accepted, sent to the person who entered the invite code.

We do not send marketing email, newsletters, promotions, or bulk campaigns. We do not buy, rent, scrape, or import address lists, and we never email an address that its owner did not type into the app themselves. Bounces and complaints are added to a suppression list automatically and are not retried.

Questions about email from Team Assist can go to appstoresupportwithasmile@icloud.com.

Who can use Team Assist

Team Assist is for adults, and athletes 15+ a parent adds.

Coaches, trainers, parents, guardians, and adult athletes use Team Assist. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or set up a family. Athletes 15 to 17 can join on their own phone with a code from their parent — they see their schedule and chat, never money, and the parent sees everything they do. Under 15 never use the app. Team Assist does not collect dates of birth or ages for any athlete.
