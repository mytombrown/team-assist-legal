Family Chores help

Need a hand?

Email appstoresupportwithasmile@icloud.com. Include your iOS version and what you were doing.

Get started

  • One parent taps Create a Family and makes a profile.
  • Add children from the Home tab. A child doesn't need a device or an Apple Account. A parent can mark chores done for them.
  • Each person who uses their own device needs an iCloud account signed in.

Invite your family

  • Tap Invite Family Member. Share the link, show the QR code, or read out the code.
  • On the other device: Join a Family, then scan the QR code or type the code.
  • If a parent already added your profile, pick it when you join. Your points and history come with you.

Chores

  • Parents make chores with points, a schedule, and who does it.
  • A chore can need a parent's approval before the points count.
  • Choose what happens if a chore is missed: skip it, roll it to the next day, or keep it open.
  • Children tap Mark Done, and add a note or number if the chore asks for one.

Points and rewards

  • Parents make rewards with a point cost.
  • Children pick a goal, and redeem when they have the points. A parent approves the request.
  • Parents can adjust a balance. A reason is always recorded.
  • The Activity tab shows every point earned, spent or changed.

Not syncing?

  • Check that iCloud is signed in on both devices.
  • Settings › Sync Now and Refresh pulls the latest.
  • Sync between devices can take a minute.

Your data

Everything lives in your family's iCloud. We collect nothing. See the privacy policy.
