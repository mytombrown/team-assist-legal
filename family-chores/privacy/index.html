Family Chores — Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026. Applies to the Family Chores app for iPhone and iPad (com.tombrown.chores) by Tom Brown.

The short version
  • We run no servers and collect nothing. No accounts of ours, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Your family's chores, rewards and points live in the family organizer's iCloud, shared only with the people who join.
  • The camera is used only to scan an invite QR code.

What the app stores

WhatWhy
Family nameTo name your family
Each member's display name, role (parent or child), avatar symbol and colorProfiles and who can do what
Chores: title, instructions, points, schedule, who it is assigned toThe chore list
Chore completions: when, an optional note or number, approval or declineApprovals and history
Rewards: title, icon, details, point cost; reward requestsSpending points
Point history: every point earned, spent, reversed or adjusted, and the reasonBalances everyone can trust

Only what you type. No email, phone number, birthday, location, contacts or photos. A child profile needs only a display name.

Where it is kept

  • On each device, in the app's own database.
  • In iCloud (CloudKit), in the private iCloud of the person who created the family. Apple runs iCloud; we cannot see it.
  • When you invite someone, iCloud shares the family with them. Everyone who joins can see and change the family's data, by their role.

Invites

  • An invite is a share link, a QR code, and a short code.
  • The short code works by saving a small lookup record in the app's public iCloud database: the code, the family name, the share link and an expiry time. It is there so a new device can find the family by code.
  • Anyone who has a valid code or link can join, so share it only with your family. Make a new code from the Invite screen if one gets out.

Camera

  • Asked for only when you tap Scan QR Code to join a family.
  • The camera reads the code on the device. No picture is saved or sent.
  • Say no and you can type the invite code instead.

What the app does NOT do

  • No servers of ours. Data goes only to iCloud, which is Apple's.
  • No third-party tracking code. No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No purchases.
  • No real money. Points are a family score, not currency.

Children

A parent sets up the family and adds children. A child's profile holds only a display name and what the family records about chores and rewards. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children. Nothing is shown to anyone outside the family.

Your choices

  • Remove a member: parents can remove a member. Their past history stays in the family's activity log.
  • Reset this device: Settings › Reset This Device signs this device out. The family's iCloud data is not touched.
  • Delete everything: the family organizer can use Settings › Delete All Family Data. It erases every chore, reward, point and member from iCloud for everyone. It cannot be undone.
  • Camera: turn it off any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera.

Changes

If a future version ever sends anything else off the device, or adds purchases, this policy will change first and the release notes will say so.
